Feature highlights

No more wrestling with 8-bit footage

Lumix S5 can record 4k 10-bit 4:2:2 up to 30p or 4k 60p* 10-bit 4:2:0, plus unlimited 4k 8-bit recording limit — all internally to an SD card.

* In APS-C or PIXEL/PIXEL mode
Apple ProRes RAW output is available with compatible recorders such as the ATOMOS Ninja V — make sure the camera is updated to version 2.0 or later

24.2MP Full-Frame CMOS Sensor with Dual-Native ISO Technology

A full-frame 24.2MP CMOS sensor allows for higher dynamic range and light sensitivity than smaller sensors such as APS-C and Micro 4/3 — the Dual-Native ISO* technology allows for even more exposure in low-light conditions.

* The two native ISO levels are at ISO 100 and 640 in Standard profile, and ISO 640 and 4000 in V-log shooting mode.

14+ stops of V-Log and V-Gamut “VariCam Look”

Shoot in V-log and retain up to 14+ stops of dynamic range. Color grade to your heart content — stretch your footage, mess around with the colors, and recreate those iconic cinematic look previously only attainable through high-end cinema cameras. Or, retain the natural, pleasing look of Lumix S5 by applying the official LUT.

6.5-stop Dual I.S. 2

Go handheld with Lumix best-in-industry in-body image stabilizer — one of the most sought-after features for run-and-gun shooters. The combined 5-axis IBIS and OIS from compatible Lumix lenses from the full-frame lineup can provide up to 6.5* stops of stabilization.

* Dual I.S 2 is available when paired with S-R24105, S-E70200, and S-R70200 — and will provide up to 6.5 stops of stabilization based on CIPA standards (Yaw/Pitch direction: f= 200mm focus distance when using S-E70200)
