About Frieren

Frieren is an elven mage who has lived for over 1,000 years. As a member of the legendary Hero Party, she journeyed alongside Himmel the Hero, Eisen the Warrior, and Heiter the Priest for ten years to defeat the Demon King.

But while that decade-long adventure changed history, for Frieren it felt like the blink of an eye. Only after watching her human companions age and pass away did she realize how much those "brief" years truly meant — and how little she had allowed herself to know them.

Now, she travels north with her apprentice Fern and the young warrior Stark, retracing the path of her old journey, hoping to understand humans and honor the memories she once took for granted.

Timeline of Major Events (up to just before Season 2):

  • (before the death of Himmel the Hero)

  • ~1000 years Frieren's village is destroyed by demons. The great mage Flamme takes her in as an apprentice and teaches her magic.
  • ~950 years After Flamme dies of old age, Frieren spends centuries wandering alone, collecting spells. (mostly "useless" or obscure ones that fascinate her, a habit that still persists)
  • 60 years Frieren joins Himmel, Eisen, and Heiter to form the Hero Party. Their quest to defeat the Demon King begins.
  • 50 years The Hero Party defeats the Demon King. They watch the Era Meteor Shower together and promise to reunite in 50 years.
  • Year 0 Frieren reunites with her aged companions to watch the meteor shower. Shortly after, Himmel dies. At his funeral, Frieren cries — realizing too late how little she knew him.

  • (after the death of Himmel the Hero)

  • 20 years Frieren visits Heiter and meets Fern, an orphan taken in by him, and begins training her.
  • ~26 years Heiter dies. Frieren and Fern set off together.
  • 28 years Frieren reunites with Eisen. After talking to him, she decides to journey to Aureole to speak with Himmel's soul. Stark joins the party.
  • 30 years Frieren and Fern take the First-Class Mage Exam. Fern passes and becomes a First-Class Mage. The party continues north.
